Your own AI helper — Gemini Gems

Stop explaining the same thing to AI every week. Explain it once, save it, and call it back with one click.

Step 1 of 4
Before you start. You need a laptop browser and a Google account (a normal Gmail works). Open gemini.google.com and sign in. Build on the laptop, not on your phone. Nothing to install.
Step 1  ·  of 4  ·  5 minutes

The same chat, every week

Most of us use AI like a search box: open a new chat, ask, copy the answer, close. Next week, start from zero again.

Why this matters. Before, every Monday you typed: “You are helping me write my weekly update for my manager. Use three headings. Keep it short. Do not invent numbers…” and only then pasted your notes. Now you write that part once, save it as a Gem, and every Monday you only paste your notes.
A Gem is just Gemini with your instructions already loaded. Same AI, same price (free), but it already knows the job before you say a word.
Think of one task you do every week (or every day) where you always have to explain the same background first. Write it on paper in one line.
Ideas from this office, if you are stuck
- Weekly update to my manager from my rough notes
- Replying to supplier or customer emails in our polite company tone
- Turning meeting notes into an action list (who, what, by when)
- Checking a delivery or inventory list against the PO
- Explaining a policy or memo in simple words for the line team
✓ Done when: you have one line on paper that starts with “Every week I…” — and you know what background you always have to repeat.
Step 2  ·  of 4  ·  10 minutes

Build one together: Weekly Update Writer

Everyone builds the same Gem first, so we all see the same screen. Your own comes in Step 3.

Watch for three things. (1) Where the instructions go. (2) The test box on the right side — you can try before you save. (3) Where the saved Gem appears afterwards.
On gemini.google.com: left side → Explore Gems (or Gems) → New Gem. In Name, type Weekly Update Writer. Then copy the block below into Instructions.
Paste into Instructions
ROLE: You help me write my weekly update to my manager.

TASK: I will paste rough notes from my week. Turn them into a clean update.

BACKGROUND: My manager reads this on the phone, so it must be short. They care most about problems that need their decision.

FORMAT:
1. Done this week (bullets, max 5)
2. Next week (bullets, max 5)
3. Need your decision (bullets; write "None" if nothing)
Under 150 words in total. Plain English.

NEVER: Never invent numbers, names or dates. If something in my notes is unclear, list it at the end under "Please check" instead of guessing.
Now test it in the preview box on the right. Paste these rough notes and press Enter.
Test notes (sample data)
finished stock count line 2 & 3, 4 cartons gloves missing still checking. trained 2 new staff on packing. forklift battery weak again, need to buy new one maybe 18k? supplier said delivery of masks moved to next fri. next week: finish line 4 count, follow up gloves
✓ Success looks like: the answer comes back with the three headings, the forklift battery sits under Need your decision, and the “18k?” shows up under Please check instead of being written as a fact. You never typed the format — the Gem already knew it.
Click Save. Close the Gem. Look at the left side under Gems.
✓ Saved when: Weekly Update Writer is in your Gems list. Click it next Monday and paste only your notes.
⚠️ No “Gems” on your screen, but you see “Skills”? Google is replacing Gems with Skills (see Step 4). Go to Settings → Skills → Create manually, use the same name, write one line in Description, paste the same Instructions, save. In any chat, type / and pick it.
⚠️ The answer ignored the format? Usually the Instructions were pasted into the chat box instead of the Instructions field. Open the Gem → Edit → check the Instructions field is full.
Step 3  ·  of 4  ·  8 minutes

Make your own

Take the one line you wrote in Step 1 and fill the same five boxes. It does not need to be beautiful. Clear is enough.

Why five boxes. When AI gives a bad answer, it is almost always because one of these was missing: it did not know who it was, what to do, what you already know, what the result should look like, or what it must never do.
Make a new Gem, give it a name that says the job, copy the template, and replace each line with your own words.
Blank five-box template
ROLE: You help me ... (who it is for, in one line)

TASK: I will paste ... Turn it into ...

BACKGROUND: (what you always have to explain: who reads it, what they care about, company rules)

FORMAT: (headings, table columns, length, language)

NEVER: Never invent numbers, names or dates. If something is unclear, list it under "Please check" instead of guessing.
💡 Keep the NEVER line. It is the line that stops AI from filling gaps with made-up facts.
✓ Success looks like: you paste one real-looking (but not confidential) input, and the answer comes back in your format on the first try. If it does not, change the Instructions — not your input — and test again.
Two more ready-made Gems you can copy
Email Reply Helper — Instructions
ROLE: You help me reply to supplier and customer emails.

TASK: I will paste the email I received, then one line saying what I want to answer. Write the reply.

BACKGROUND: Our tone is polite, direct and short. We do not over-apologise. We confirm numbers exactly as written in the original email.

FORMAT: Subject line, then the reply. Max 120 words. End with "Best regards," and leave my name blank.

NEVER: Never promise prices, dates or quantities that are not in my one-line answer. If the email asks something I did not answer, list it under "Please check".
Meeting Action List — Instructions
ROLE: You turn my meeting notes into an action list.

TASK: I will paste rough notes or a transcript. Pull out every action.

BACKGROUND: The list is sent to everyone after the meeting, so each action needs one owner.

FORMAT: A table with columns: No. | Action | Owner | Due date. Below the table, a short "Decisions made" list.

NEVER: Never guess an owner or due date. If it was not said, write "?" in that cell and list it under "Please check".
Step 4  ·  of 4  ·  2 minutes

Three rules, and one change coming

Read these before you share a Gem or add files to it.

Rule 1 — No confidential files in Knowledge. A Gem can hold files (the Knowledge section). Why it is risky: the file stays in your Google account, and anyone you share the Gem with can see the instructions and the files. What to do: never upload contracts, price lists, salary data or personal data. Use a sample or a version with names removed.
Rule 2 — You check the answer. AI does the work; you must be able to say why the answer is right. Read the “Please check” list every time before you send anything.
Rule 3 — Keep a copy of your Instructions. The Instructions are the real asset, not the button. Save them in a document. The same text works in any AI tool — in ChatGPT, paste it at the top of a new chat.
The changeWhat it means for you
Google is replacing Gems with SkillsSame idea: saved instructions you call again. In a normal chat you type / and pick the Skill. You can use more than one at a time.
Personal Google accounts: from 17 November 2026Your Gems are moved to Skills automatically. Name, instructions and supported files come with them. Nothing you build today is wasted.
Company (Google Workspace) accounts: laterGems keep working until at least March 2027.
